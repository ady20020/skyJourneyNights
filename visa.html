Multiple-entry options

30-day and 60-day multiple-entry visas are usually priced higher than single-entry — indicative totals often land around AED 700 – 1,200 (30-day multi) and AED 900 – 1,700+ (60-day multi), depending on provider.

Ask for multi-entry quote

Typical add-ons

  • Express / urgent processing — often +AED 300 – 600 vs standard timing
  • Travel insurance — commonly ~AED 50 – 300 depending on cover
  • VIP / assisted submission — optional service fees vary by agency

Good to know

  • Rates change with ICP / immigration rules and seasonal demand.
  • Some nationalities follow different document or fee rules.
  • Final approval is always issued by UAE authorities — we prepare and submit professionally.
